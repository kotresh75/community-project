## 2. Report-Ready Objectives Section

### 2.1 Primary Aim
To design, develop, and evaluate a non-commercial, multilingual **Community Renewable Energy Awareness and Management Portal** that simplifies renewable energy information and promotes the adoption of sustainable energy practices within communities.

### 2.2 Specific Objectives
1. **O1 — Baseline Field & Stakeholder Assessment:** To interact with local community stakeholders and conduct a preliminary survey to identify real awareness gaps, current household energy habits, and practical barriers to adopting renewable and sustainable energy practices.
2. **O2 — Multilingual Open Public Awareness:** To design and publish an open, non-commercial, **10-language** (*English + 9 major Indian regional languages*) knowledge hub covering renewable energy resources (solar, biogas, clean cooking, energy conservation) and simplified public support schemes without login barriers.
3. **O3 — Personal Sustainable-Practice Management:** To promote household-level adoption of sustainable energy practices through actionable readiness checklists, personal adoption pledges, progress tracking, and a secondary user-input practice impact estimator.
4. **O4 — Community Initiative & Authentic Adoption Management:** To enable community members and coordinators to organize and participate in awareness drives, coordinate local sustainable-energy initiatives, and track authentic community adoption strictly from real user submissions.
5. **O5 — Testing, Usability & Impact Evaluation:** To verify the portal's functionality and evaluate its usability, **multilingual clarity**, and potential societal impact through structured testing and stakeholder feedback.
